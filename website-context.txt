My strong recommendation: **do not make “treatment” the main ad word for paralysis.** It is more influential, yes — but it also increases ad rejection and legal/compliance risk.

For India, the **Drugs and Magic Remedies (Objectionable Advertisements) Act** restricts advertisements that suggest drugs/remedies for the diagnosis, cure, mitigation, treatment, or prevention of listed diseases, and **“Paralysis” is listed in the Schedule**. The Act also prohibits misleading drug-related claims. 
Google also restricts healthcare and prescription-drug-related promotions, and Meta does not allow ads that imply the viewer personally has a medical condition. ([Google Help][1])

So, **“treatment” is not impossible**, but I would avoid using it aggressively in ads and hero headlines.
