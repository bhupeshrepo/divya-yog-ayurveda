Paralysis · Lakwa

Ayurvedic Care for Paralysis / Lakwa Recovery Support

Paralysis — commonly called Lakwa — can affect movement, strength, speech, facial control, walking and daily independence. We offer Ayurvedic consultation and supportive care planning for people recovering from paralysis-related weakness.

The consultation looks at your condition, medical history, body strength, digestion, mobility, sleep and daily routine — then builds personalized Ayurvedic guidance around it.

Book Consultation

We provide consultation for

  • Paralysis recovery support
  • One-side body weakness
  • Facial paralysis / facial weakness support
  • Post-stroke weakness support
  • Reduced movement or stiffness
  • Weakness in hand, leg, shoulder or face
  • Lifestyle & diet guidance during recovery
  • Long-term wellness & strength support

Important medical note: If weakness is sudden, or there is face drooping, speech difficulty, confusion, vision trouble, loss of balance or sudden severe headache — visit a hospital immediately. Do not delay emergency care.

Migraine · Headache

Ayurvedic Consultation for Migraine & Recurrent Headache

Migraine is not just a normal headache. Many people have repeated episodes with nausea, sensitivity to light or sound, head heaviness, neck stiffness, disturbed sleep, acidity, stress or food-triggered discomfort.

We provide consultation by understanding your routine, digestion, sleep, stress level, food habits, screen time and body constitution — then focus on identifying likely triggers and correcting them.
