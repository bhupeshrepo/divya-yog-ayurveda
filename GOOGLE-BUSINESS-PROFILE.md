# Google Business Profile — Setup Pack
### Divya Yog Ayurvedic Chikitsalaya, Rohtak

A free Google Business Profile (GBP) is **as important as the website** for a local clinic — it's what shows up in Google Maps and in the "near me" results when someone searches *ayurvedic clinic Rohtak*. It also powers **location extensions** in Google Ads and lets patients call/WhatsApp/get-directions in one tap.

> "Google Business Manager" = **Google Business Profile**. Create/manage it at **https://business.google.com** (or search your clinic name on Google and click *"Own this business?"*). The *Business Profile Manager* dashboard is only needed if you manage multiple locations.

## 8. Reviews = local ranking fuel
- After each satisfied consultation, ask the patient to leave a Google review.
- Get your **short review link**: GBP dashboard → *Ask for reviews* → copy link (looks like `https://g.page/r/…`). Save it and send on WhatsApp.
- **Always reply** to every review (thank them; for any concern, respond calmly and move details offline). Reply in Hindi or English to match the reviewer.
- ⚠️ Never offer money/discounts for reviews, and don't post fake ones — Google removes them and can suspend the profile.

---

## 9. Verification
Google will verify you own the business — usually by **phone/SMS, video call, or postcard**. For a clinic, video verification is common: you'll record a short clip showing the signboard, the street, and inside the clinic. Keep the signboard and address visible. Until verified, edits won't show publicly.

---

## 10. Once verified — connect it to ads
- In **Google Ads → Assets → Location** → link this Business Profile so your ads show the address, a call button and directions (higher clicks, lower cost).
- Keep NAP (**N**ame, **A**ddress, **P**hone) **identical** on the website, GBP, and ads — Google trusts consistent info and ranks it higher locally.

*All wording here follows the same ad-safe rules as the website: no "100% cure / guaranteed / permanent", focus on "care / consultation / recovery support" — aligned with Google's healthcare policy and India's Drugs & Magic Remedies Act (paralysis is a Schedule-listed condition).*
